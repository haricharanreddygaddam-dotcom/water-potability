About Water Potability & Random Forest Classification

Machine Learning Architecture

Trained on multi-dimensional water quality datasets consisting of physical, chemical, and biological indicators. Employs an ensemble of 150 Decision Trees with bootstrap aggregation and Gini impurity splitting to capture non-linear contaminant interactions.

WHO & EPA Safety Guidelines

World Health Organization guidelines require strict limits on disinfection byproducts (Trihalomethanes < 80 μg/L, Chloramines < 4 ppm), optimal biological pH (6.5 – 8.5), and low turbidity (< 5 NTU) to ensure pathogen-free drinking water.

Interactive 3D Visualizer

Built on Three.js WebGL physics, dynamically altering fluid absorption, refraction, particulate scattering, effervescence, and molecular bonds in real-time corresponding to chemical input values.
